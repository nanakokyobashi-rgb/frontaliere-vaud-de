Vollständige Beschreibung

Location: Orbe, Switzerland Work model: Hybrid Contract type / duration: Internship, 6 months Activity rate: 100% Start date: February–March 2027, flexible

Be a force for good and transform your potential into something extraordinary.

As a Decision AI & Reinforcement Learning Intern at the Nestlé Deep Tech Center , your mission will be to research and prototype AI methods for sequential decision-making in complex industrial systems.

You will explore how intelligent agents can learn, predict, plan and optimize decisions in simulated industrial environments. Your work will help the team understand which Decision AI approaches can create practical value, from optimizing daily operations to improving the design and configuration of industrial systems.

You will join the Artificial Intelligence & Digital Group in Orbe, working in a collaborative Deep Tech environment where advanced AI, machine learning, robotics, sensor technologies and digital twins are used to shape the future of food and manufacturing at scale. This is an opportunity to turn emerging academic research into applied prototypes while developing hands-on experience at the frontier of reinforcement learning and Decision AI.

How you’ll make an impact as a Decision AI & Reinforcement Learning Intern

  • Design and extend simulation environments representing industrial processes.
  • Build end-to-end experimentation pipelines covering model training, evaluation, logging and reproducibility
  • Prototype and benchmark decision-making approaches, from heuristic and optimization baselines to model-free and model-based reinforcement learning
  • Explore advanced methods such as world models, predictive latent representations, offline reinforcement learning, Decision Transformers and multi-agent reinforcement learning.
  • Use simulation to evaluate operational decisions and alternative system configurations, including resource allocation and task assignment
  • Perform ablation, robustness, generalization and data-efficiency tests to evaluate different approaches rigorously
  • Follow relevant research in reinforcement learning and sequential decision-making and translate promising ideas into applied prototypes
  • Communicate technical findings clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders

What will make you successful

  • Bachelor or Master’s student or recent graduate in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Applied Mathematics, Engineering or a related field
  • Solid Python skills and hands-on experience with machine learning frameworks such as PyTorch or JAX
  • Foundations in reinforcement learning and sequential decision-making, such as Markov Decision Processes
  • Experimental mindset: comfortable debugging code, working with data and using Git
  • Curious, autonomous and proactive, with strong problem-solving skills
  • Familiarity with offline or model-based reinforcement learning, world models, multi-agent systems or operations research is a plus
  • Professional working proficiency in English
