Vollständige Beschreibung

NICE TO MEET YOU! IN SHORT Customer satisfaction is at the heart of everything we do, and we are looking for someone who takes pride in making people happy with delicious food at the workplace. As our new Customer Service Agent (hourly contract at min. 24h/week) in Lausanne, you will be the main point of contact for our customers and ensure they feel well taken care of in every interaction. Step into a dynamic, hybrid role where our service and technology continuously strive for excellence! YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

  • You are the first point of contact for our customers, handling incoming emails, feedback, and calls professionally and efficiently. You live by our motto: “There is always a solution, never a problem.”
  • Process and resolve customer inquiries in a structured way to ensure proactive, long-term quality improvement.
  • Improve our customer service processes continuously to help take FELFEL to the next level of service.
  • Exchange ideas with team members and share know-how to maintain consistent, high-quality customer support.
  • Collaborate across different FELFEL departments, such as Hardware, Operations, and Quality Control, to broaden your understanding of our processes. WHAT TO EXPECT YOUR PROFILE
  • Education or practical experience in business administration, communication, hospitality, tourism, or a similar field.
  • Strong problem-solving skills with a genuine drive to delight people and provide excellent service.
  • High motivation to learn, grow, and take on responsibility from day one.
  • Passion for good food and the ability to speak enthusiastically about our culinary offerings to inspire others.
  • Fluent French and English skills
  • German is a strong plus.
  • Non-smoker - we don't want you to smell like cigarettes, neither at work nor at customer meetings. After all, FELFEL is about good food.
  • Valid work permit or Swiss passport. YOU WILL BE MEASURED BY OUR BENEFITS
  • Hourly rate of CHF 25.00 with regular working shifts.
  • Enjoy fresh, high-quality meals just steps away from your workspace, every day.
  • Fuel your day with unlimited barista-style coffee and premium tea, thanks to our Gavetti machine.
  • Internal Customer Excellence training and valuable insights into Operations, Customer Excellence, and Sustainability.
  • Responsibility from day one with a high level of independence.
  • Work in a collaborative environment that values initiative and gives you the space to contribute your ideas. Does this sound like the job for you? We can't wait to learn more about you! APPLY NOW with your complete documents in either French or English. APPLY NOW ABOUT US FELFEL VALUES
