Vollständige Beschreibung

Mission

We are seeking a postdoctoral researcher specializing in RF/mm-wave integrated circuit design to develop RF electronics for a novel wafer-scale ion accelerator. The project combines RF integrated circuits, microsystems, and ion-beam technology within a compact, 3D-integrated platform. Working in close collaboration with the PhD student responsible for the accelerator structure, the candidate will define and develop RF power generation and distribution architectures for microscale accelerating and focusing electrodes, addressing challenges in high-frequency power delivery and 3D integration.

Tasks

  • Define and compare RF power generation and distribution architectures for the accelerator.
  • Investigate and design RF/mm-wave amplification and power generation circuits operating at 10–30 GHz.
  • Develop impedance-matching networks and RF distribution solutions for capacitive loads.
  • Optimize RF power delivery, efficiency, and amplitude/phase control while assessing thermal constraints.
  • Perform RF characterization, including VNA, S-parameter, power, and phase measurements.
  • Design and, depending on project maturity, contribute to the fabrication and experimental validation of an RFIC demonstrator.

Profile

Required Qualifications

  • PhD in Electrical Engineering, Microelectronics, RF Engineering, or a related field.
  • Strong background in analog/RFIC design, preferably at mm-wave frequencies.
  • Experience in RF power amplifier design and/or high-frequency matching networks.
  • Proficiency with Cadence Virtuoso, Keysight ADS, or equivalent.
  • Understanding of RF parasitics, impedance matching, and electromagnetic effects.
  • Ability to work independently and within a multidisciplinary research team.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience in mm-wave power amplifiers, including high-efficiency or switching architectures.
  • Previous IC tape-out experience, including design, layout, or characterization.
  • Experience with EM simulation tools (HFSS, Momentum, CST, or equivalent).
  • Hands-on experience with RF/mm-wave characterization and VNA measurements.
  • Knowledge of high-output-swing RF circuits, stacked-transistor architectures, or power combining.
  • Experience with CMOS, SiGe BiCMOS, or other RF/mm-wave semiconductor technologies.
  • Familiarity with TSVs, 3D integration, RF packaging, or capacitive RF loads.

Research environment

The successful candidate will join NanoLab at EPFL, contributing to an interdisciplinary project combining RFIC design, microfabrication, and particle accelerator technology. The position offers opportunities to explore novel RF architectures and develop an integrated accelerator demonstrator in collaboration.
